**ADBZero** is a high-performance, browser-based Android management suite. No installation, no complicated drivers, and 100% client-side security. Control, debloat, and optimize your Android device directly from your Chrome, Edge, or Opera browser. Any Chromium-based browser is supported, including Brave, Zen, Vivaldi and others.
Firefox and Safari not supported. Sorry but this don't depend on us, it's the lack of WebUSB support in those browsers.

## 🌟 Key Features & Functionality

### 📱 Dashboard & Device Insights
Get a comprehensive overview of your device's technical specifications.
- **How to use**: Simply connect your device via USB. The dashboard automatically populates with Android version, API level, battery status, resolution, and hardware specs.
- **Benefit**: Instantly verify device identity and health before performing advanced operations.

### 🧹 Universal Debloater (Community Driven)
Remove unwanted system apps (bloatware) that slow down your phone and drain battery.
- **How to use**: Navigate to the **Debloater** tab. You can search for specific packages or use **Community Lists** to see what others are removing.
- **Root Mode**: For advanced users, enable Root Mode for permanent system-level removal.
- **Method**: Select apps from the list and hit "Disable" or "Uninstall". ADBZero handles the sequential execution.

### 🛡️ De-Google Levels
Privacy-focused removal of Google services, from minimal tracking blocks to a total "De-Google" experience.
- **How to use**: Go to **De-Google** and choose your level (Essential, Low, Medium, High, or Total).
- **FOSS Alternatives**: The tool suggests Open Source alternatives (like Aurora Store or F-Droid) to replace removed Google services.

### 📺 Screen Mirroring & Remote Control
Low-latency remote control of your device with adaptive quality.
- **How to use**: Click the **Screen Mirror** tab and hit "Start".
- **Features**: Clicks send taps, adaptive bitrate ensures smoothness, and you can even record your screen directly to your PC.

### 🏗️ App Cloner (Native Sandboxing)
Clone apps using Android's native Managed Profiles (Work Profiles) for zero performance impact.
- **How to use**: Open **App Cloner**, set up the profile, and select which apps to "Clone".
- **Benefit**: Isolated data and separate app instances without the overhead of virtual machines.

### 📂 Advanced File Manager
Root-enabled file explorer for deep system access.
- **How to use**: Use **User Mode** for standard files or **Root Mode** for system partitions.
- **Operations**: Upload, download, rename, delete, and even change permissions (`chmod`) or ownership (`chown`).

### 📦 Independent App Store & APK Installer
A secure way to discover F-Droid apps and install local APKs effortlessly.
- **How to use**: Browse the **Store** for trusted open-source apps or drag-and-drop your own APKs into the **APK Installer**.
- **Batch Install**: Queue multiple APKs and install them all in one session.

### 🛠️ Device & Privacy Tools
Quick toggles for DPI, resolution, animations, and Private DNS.
- **How to use**: Adjust sliders for DPI/Density or choose a **Private DNS** provider (AdGuard, NextDNS) to block ads system-wide without root.

## 🚀 Getting Started

To keep things simple and secure, we don't require you to download any software to your PC.

### 🌐 Direct Access
Available on **[www.adbzero.com](https://adbzero.com)**

### 📖 Usage Steps
1. **Enable USB Debugging** on your Android device (Settings > Developer Options).
2. **Connect via USB** to a Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Edge, Opera).
3. **Authorize** the connection on your phone popup.
4. **Enjoy** a faster, cleaner, and more private Android experience!
